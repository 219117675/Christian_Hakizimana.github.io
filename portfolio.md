## 📁 Agile Project Portfolio (FundiShop)

> **The five sections are connected and work together to tell the complete story of the project, showing how an initial idea develops into a planned and organised solution.**  
> The **Charter** establishes the foundation by explaining the project's purpose, objectives, scope, and vision. Building on this foundation, the **Stories** translate the project's objectives into specific user needs and explain the features the system must provide. These stories then feed directly into the **Backlogs**, where the requirements are broken down into manageable tasks, prioritised, and organised for development. The **Logbook** completes the process by documenting how the planned tasks are carried out, recording progress, challenges, decisions, and completed activities. In this way, each section connects to the next, demonstrating how the project moves from initial planning to requirements gathering, task organisation, implementation, and continuous progress tracking.
